## Context

Dotscreen is a Paris-based studio that builds streaming and broadcast apps for
Smart TVs, set-top boxes, and connected devices - the kind of software that
ships on a customer's living-room hardware and has to work with a remote, not
a touchscreen.

## What I did

I worked across a rolling portfolio of client apps for major international
broadcasters: Disney, Arte, France.tv, beIN Sports, Mediaset, STARZPLAY,
Deezer, OCS, each with its own deadlines, device targets, and quirks.

The work was a mix of:

- React for the newer generations of smart TV platforms
- QML (Qt / C++) for lower-level embedded UIs on set-top boxes
- Embedding and configuring media players for video, audio, and live streaming

The hard part wasn't the code so much as the fragmentation. Smart TVs are a
zoo: old WebKit engines, underpowered CPUs, remote-control focus management,
DRM quirks, and a dozen subtly different platforms (Samsung Tizen, LG webOS,
Android TV, various set-top box OSes). Cross-device compatibility and
performance were the core engineering constraints on every single project.
